About the studio

Designing spaces with purpose.

Dhananjay Malhotra Architects is a multidisciplinary design practice working across architecture, interiors and landscapes. The studio focuses on creating customized spaces that respond to each client's aspirations, requirements and way of life.

We are a team of specialized Architects and Interior Designers, working with thoughtful design methods to deliver spaces tailored to our clients' requirements.
